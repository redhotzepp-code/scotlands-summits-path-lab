Ground symbols

Contour heights use native 50 m indexed lines. No automatic contour rescanning.

Extra online names are optional. Tap the button to look them up; there are no automatic background lookups.

Forest boundaries and scattered rock symbols are unchanged. Rivers and burns are not guaranteed safe crossings. OSM coverage is incomplete: missing features do NOT prove safe ground.

Named hill heights

Hill names include a smaller height beneath them when the source records a summit elevation in metres. No estimated or invented values.

Paths and tracks

Cartography: estate and vehicle tracks use two dark edges around a pale centre; walking and stalkers’ paths use one dark dashed line. Both begin at the 1 km scale.

All mapped footpaths, trails, stalkers' paths, cycleways, estate tracks and service tracks become visible at approximately the 1 km scale. Linework gets clearer as you zoom in; detailed OpenStreetMap route data helps fill gaps in the basemap. A mapped line does not guarantee safe ground or access.

The approved R7 hillshade, contour, rock, forest and bog styling is restored. River Tilt path geometry is bundled as a verified static dataset; no live path queries. Other regions still depend on existing basemap route coverage.
